About

Javvin Company is an independent publisher based in San Francisco, specializing in books and posters on technological innovation. More than twenty years ago, Javvin's printed technology books and knowledge posters were beloved by readers; today, Javvin returns with a digital-first approach, publishing every work in both Chinese and English editions. The ebooks and digital posters sold here are delivered as high-resolution PDF files, available for download immediately after purchase. Print editions are available via print-on-demand on Amazon and other websites.

Contact: javvincompany@gmail.com

Return Policy

All sales of digital products (ebooks and digital posters) are final upon completion of delivery; delivered digital products are non-refundable. We guarantee successful delivery of your files — if a download fails or a file is corrupted, we will re-deliver it at no additional charge. Print editions are sold through third-party retailers, and returns and refunds for print editions are governed by the policies of the retailer from which the print edition was purchased.
