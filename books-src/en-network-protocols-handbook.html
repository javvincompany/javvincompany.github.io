Network Protocols Handbook: Understanding Connectivity Across the Internet, Cloud, and AI Systems

by Jielin Dong · Javvin Company

Fifth Edition · English · 324 pages · ISBN: 978-1-60267-032-7 · January 2027

Network Protocols Handbook, Fifth Edition follows a request from a device to a cloud service or an AI data center and back, and explains the protocols at each step: Ethernet, Wi-Fi, IP, routing, and DNS; TCP, QUIC, and HTTP; encryption and identity; container and cloud networks.

Dedicated chapters cover AI applications and infrastructure: streaming responses and API delivery, the protocols agents use to call tools and one another (MCP and A2A), and the networks behind distributed training and inference, including collective communication, RDMA, and Ultra Ethernet.

Annotated packet examples, troubleshooting methods, and companion packet-analysis exercises show how protocols appear on the wire and how to diagnose them when they fail. A subject index and protocol reference support both study and quick lookup.

First published in 2004, the Handbook is written for network engineers, developers, security practitioners, and students.
